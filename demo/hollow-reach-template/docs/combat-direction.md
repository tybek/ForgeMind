# Hollow Reach: Combat Direction

Field information creates combat advantage: sound, sightlines, heat warnings,
and patrol movement help the player choose when to engage or withdraw.

Enemy pressure should come from positioning, coordination, and changing goals
rather than inflated health pools. Faction patrols pursue their own objectives
and can collide with each other without the player starting the encounter.

## Decision

Enemy difficulty will come primarily from tactical decisions and coordination,
not raw hit-point scaling.

## Open Question

How much warning should the player receive before two faction patrols collide?

Maybe a later prototype could let the player remotely trigger old geothermal
vents as battlefield hazards. This is a proposed future idea, not a decided
mechanic or durable fact.
