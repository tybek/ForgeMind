# Hollow Reach: World and Factions

Hollow Reach is a remote industrial settlement surrounding geothermal wells,
maintenance tunnels, and a damaged heat-exchange complex. Salvaged machine
parts, batteries, and pressure-safe tools form the settlement's practical
economy.

The Reach Salvage Compact is a civilian organization that marks recovery zones,
negotiates salvage rights, and maintains shared route beacons. Its authority is
useful but incomplete beyond the inhabited ring.
